What we do

One plan, not three. With every lane set to the same milestones.

Every company runs in more than one lane. In medtech it is regulatory, finance and commercial. In a growing company it is the operating plan and the model. In a transaction it is the forecast and what diligence finds.

Each lane can be sound on its own and still not add up. Good advice arrives one lane at a time. We help you build one plan you can act on with confidence.

Value is lost in the gaps between the lanes.

Investors, boards and buyers look for one plan that holds together across the lanes and across the milestones.

That is progress that is easy to explain and easy to recognise.

A venture investor is looking for a reason to say no, and often finds it in the first few minutes. Every stakeholder, from the board to a buyer, needs to hear the same story in their own terms. When the lanes tell different stories, the gaps show when the stakes are highest.

The KSC milestone model

From the plan to the market. No gaps.

The market you choose sets your evidence, your regulatory pathway and your capital decisions. We work across the whole model: the plan that sets the claims, the evidence, the pathway and the raise, and the build that turns a cleared product into revenue.

The plan is delivered as a series of milestones. Each milestone brings in the specialists needed to reach it, and is measured by the risk it removes and what the company is worth once it is reached.

Investors do not fund a company to stand still. They fund the milestones that reduce investor risk and make it more valuable for the next round. The process is the same before regulatory approval or at first revenue: always the next milestone, working towards who will use the device and who will pay for it.

The raise

We prepare companies to raise. The CEO makes the case.

Investors invest in you and your ideas. From pre-seed through Series B, on rounds from £500,000 to more than £50 million, our job is to make sure both are ready when you walk into the room.

The investor's read

Every investor has requirements of their own. We know both sides of the table. We test the company the way your investor will: the market and its competition, the problem a customer will pay to solve, and the team that has to carry the plan. Whatever an investor would find, you find first.

Ready to raise

While the CEO has to make the case, we make sure the company and the team are ready to make it: investor-grade materials, documentation that holds up in diligence, and a team prepared for the questions we know investors will ask.

How we prepare a company

When a first meeting does not lead to a second, it is rarely the idea. Usually it is a mismatch between the founder and the plan, or the raise and the model. We align the vision, the message and the numbers, so diligence confirms the story.

Every founder has heard it.
Most at every round.

“Come back when you have more traction.” Pre-clinical, newly cleared, letters of interest in hand: there is always one more thing.

What counts as traction changes with the stage. Before revenue, traction is evidence, built in order: verification, clinical results, contact with the regulator. Commercial traction is a customer who pays for the product, and pays again.

Investors ask for more when they do not yet understand the market or the company. We prepare the founder and the team to answer both: to show where the company stands today, and to make that case with confidence.
