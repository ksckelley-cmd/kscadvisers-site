The correct specialist

Some work belongs to a specialist. We qualify and recommend the firms, in the UK and the US.

These disciplines carry professional duties of their own, and the company's plan is stronger for having them in it. We do not deliver this work. The specialists work to the company's plan, alongside its other advisers. Their work and ours answer to the same plan, and it is the company's.
