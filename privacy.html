Privacy notice

KSC Advisers Ltd advises founders and chief executives of medical technology companies. This notice says what personal data we hold, why, where it comes from, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do. It applies under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 and, for people in Ireland and the rest of the EU, under the EU GDPR.

Who we are

KSC Advisers Ltd, registered in Scotland, company number SC905080, 9 Ainslie Place, Edinburgh EH3 6AS. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office, reference ZC271397. Questions and requests about your data go to request@kscadvisers.com.

What we hold and why

We hold business contact data about people we may work with, refer to, or meet in their professional capacity: founders, executives, investors, accelerator and government staff, and people at partner firms. That is your name, job title and organisation; your business email, business phone and LinkedIn profile; how and where we met or heard of you; notes of our conversations and meetings and where you sit in our pipeline; and the calendar entries and email threads that our assistant tools read to prepare our day.

We hold it to develop and manage business relationships: to introduce our work, follow up on conversations, make introductions and keep in touch about the field. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in doing so. We do not rely on consent for this. We do not record special category data (health, ethnicity, beliefs and the like); if it reaches us, we delete it.

Where it comes from

From you directly; from an introduction; from an event we both attended; from public professional profiles such as LinkedIn or Companies House; and from lists provided by referral partners. Where we did not get your details from you, we send you this notice with our first message. Until then, this published notice is how we tell you.

Email

One-to-one business emails to you in your work role carry an opt-out in every message. Bulk mailings such as newsletters or event invitations go to company addresses only, with an unsubscribe in every message. If you tell us to stop, we stop at once.

Where it is held and who processes it

Our records are held in Microsoft SharePoint, with data at rest in the United Kingdom, under Microsoft's data protection terms. Our email and calendar are held in Google Workspace, under Google's data processing terms. We use AI services from established providers to read and maintain our records and to prepare our work; each processes data on our behalf under its commercial data processing terms. Where a provider processes data outside the UK, the transfer is covered by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the EU standard contractual clauses. We never sell personal data and never share it outside the firm except with these processors.

Client files

If you are a client, the files you send us are used only to do your work, never for training or benchmarking, and are kept in a folder of their own. Where a file you send names other people, for example shareholders on a cap table, that is your data and your responsibility; we hold it as you agree and pass any request from those people to you. We do not accept patient or trial-subject data.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to our holding it. Email request@kscadvisers.com. We answer within one month. Objection to marketing is absolute: we stop at once. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or, if you are in Ireland, to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

Changes

This notice is dated 23 November 2026. We update it when our practice changes and the current version is always at this address.
