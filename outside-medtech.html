Situations outside medtech

Do you work with companies outside of medtech?

Our work clusters by situation, not by sector.

The moments that decide a company's course recur across markets: a raise, a launch, a restructuring, a move across borders, an exit. We bring the commercial, operating and finance judgement of having led a diverse set of companies through their inflection points. You bring the domain expertise. We help you execute. We are in your corner.

The same seven steps

Almost every company follows the same seven steps. Regulation adds a layer to each one.

An idea that solves a problem. How big the problem is. Develop the solution, finance it, make it, distribute it, sell and market it. The steps are the same in any company. The regulatory layer is what turns a basic step into a decision with a cost, a timeline and a specialist attached, and it is why the order of the decisions matters.

Regulation reaches every industry; it is a matter of degree. In e-commerce it is consumer law and advertising standards, and the market remains the judge that matters, answering in weeks. In medtech a regulator, a committee and a payer each have to answer first, and each answer takes years and a specialist.

The market view

Medtech innovation often goes off target from the beginning when it lacks a cohesive market view, and each new layer of regulation hides it further.
